Sky Stack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

The Sky Stack app posts gameplay clips to the game's own TikTok account. It is used only by the Sky Stack developer.

What we access

When the developer signs in with TikTok, the app receives basic profile information and permission to upload videos to that same account. It does not read other users' data.

What we store

The sign-in token is kept on the developer's own computer in encrypted system storage. We do not sell, share or publish it, and we collect no data from viewers or players through this app.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting data

The developer can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok settings under Security and permissions, which removes the token's use.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.

Contact

Message the developer through the Sky Stack page on Roblox.
